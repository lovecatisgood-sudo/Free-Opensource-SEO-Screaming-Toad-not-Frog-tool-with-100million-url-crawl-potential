# Lesson 2 — Why Websites Invest in SEO and ASO

**Target duration:** 4 minutes

## Narration

[On screen: Useful page → search discovery → qualified visitor → lead or sale.]

Businesses invest in SEO because discoverability can compound. A useful page may attract qualified visitors repeatedly. Technical SEO can also protect existing value during a redesign, domain migration, ecommerce expansion, or multilingual launch.

But SEO is not one standardized product. A one-time technical audit is different from ongoing content, local SEO, digital PR, conversion work, or AI-search research. Pricing changes by country, competition, site size, expertise, and whether implementation is included.

Agency surveys provide a broad reference. Common ranges include approximately 50 to 100 US dollars per hour, 500 to 2,000 dollars for a project, and 500 to 1,000 dollars for a monthly package. Experienced consultants and large or complex projects may charge much more. These are market observations, not a recommended DJAI price list.

[On screen: Audit ≠ finished service. Audit → prioritize → implement → verify.]

What does a good client actually pay for? Not a spreadsheet containing hundreds of warnings. They pay for diagnosis, prioritization, implementation, risk reduction, and verification. An expert recognizes when 200 findings have one template-level cause, when a warning is harmless, and when a single broken canonical affects valuable pages.

SEO Screaming Toad reduces the cost of collecting technical evidence because the software is free and open source. An AI coding agent can also reduce the technical barrier to operating the crawler and applying fixes. Neither replaces judgment.

The professional promise should be clear: we will audit an agreed scope, show evidence, explain limitations, implement approved improvements, and measure what changed. We will not guarantee a ranking, traffic level, revenue number, rich result, or AI citation.

[On screen: “Sell verified work—not magic.”]
