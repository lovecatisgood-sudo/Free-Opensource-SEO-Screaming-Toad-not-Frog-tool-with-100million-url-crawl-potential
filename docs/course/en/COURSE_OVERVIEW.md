# Course 4: SEO & ASO with AI Agent — Top Rank Your Site on Search

English script package — review draft 2
Prepared for the DJAI Academy online community
Product referenced: SEO Screaming Toad `2.0.0-rc.4`
Target course duration: **60 minutes**

## Course promise

This fast-paced course teaches students to understand SEO and AI Search Optimization, run an authorized technical audit with SEO Screaming Toad, operate the crawler through an AI agent using MCP, prioritize evidence, fix selected problems, and verify the result with a recrawl.

“Top rank your site” describes the learner's goal. No crawler, coding agent, or technical fix can guarantee rankings, indexing, traffic, conversions, rich results, or AI citations. The course teaches how to build and verify a stronger technical search foundation.

## Audience

Website owners, marketers, SEO beginners, developers, AI-assisted builders, and technical SEO practitioners.

## Folder structure

Each lesson has its own folder and `SCRIPT.md`. Each module ends with a rapid `MODULE_QUIZ.md`. The course ends with `FINAL_QUIZ.md`.

## Production convention

- Text under **Narration** is intended for Omnivoice.
- Text in `[On screen]` brackets is direction for HyperFrames.
- Demonstrations should use a purpose-built fixture or a small website the instructor is authorized to crawl.
- Show genuine tool output. Blur private paths, tokens, client data, and local usernames.
- Recheck time-sensitive prices and product features immediately before recording.

## Required claim language

- In this course, **ASO means AI Search Optimization**, not App Store Optimization. AEO and GEO are related industry terms.
- Toad covers much of the core workflow used in normal technical website audits; it does not claim complete Screaming Frog feature parity.
- Approved scale language: **“Architecturally designed for segmented campaigns beyond 100 million URLs; this is a theoretical scalability target, not a tested, supported, or guaranteed capacity.”**
- A clean crawl supports a strong technical SEO baseline. It does not guarantee a search or business outcome.

## Completion

Recommended passing requirement: at least 8 of 10 answers correct on the final quiz, plus completion of one authorized crawl and a written explanation of one evidence-backed issue.
