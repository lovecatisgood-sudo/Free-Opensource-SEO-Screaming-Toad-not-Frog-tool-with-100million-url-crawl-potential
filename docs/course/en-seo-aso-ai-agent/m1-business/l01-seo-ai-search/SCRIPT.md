# Lesson 1 — What SEO and AI Search Optimization Mean

**Target duration:** 4 minutes

## Narration

[On screen: Course title, DJAI Academy logo, and SEO Screaming Toad logo.]

Welcome to SEO and ASO with AI Agent, created for the DJAI Academy community.

SEO means Search Engine Optimization. It is the work of helping search systems discover, crawl, understand, and present useful website content to the right people. Good SEO is not keyword repetition. It combines a technically accessible website with clear organization, helpful content, good user experience, and trustworthy evidence.

A search engine normally needs to discover a URL, crawl it, interpret its signals, and decide whether it should enter an index. These stages are different. A page can be discovered but blocked from crawling. It can be crawled but marked `noindex`. It can be indexed but still fail to rank for a competitive search.

[On screen: Discover → Crawl → Understand → Index → Present.]

In this course, ASO means **AI Search Optimization**. Do not confuse it with App Store Optimization. You may also hear AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization, and GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization.

AI-search optimization asks how website information can be retrieved, understood, and potentially referenced by AI-assisted search experiences. The foundation still looks familiar: crawlable pages, clear headings, consistent entities, useful internal links, original information, visible evidence, and technically correct delivery.

Google's current guidance says its foundational SEO practices remain relevant to generative AI features. That does not mean an SEO tool can guarantee an AI citation. A crawler can inspect technical preconditions; it cannot see every private ranking or retrieval system.

[On screen: “Strong foundation, not guaranteed outcome.”]

Our workflow throughout this course is simple: collect evidence, understand the cause, make a safe fix, and recrawl to verify it. “Top rank your site” is our ambition—not a promise that any tool can manufacture a number-one position.
