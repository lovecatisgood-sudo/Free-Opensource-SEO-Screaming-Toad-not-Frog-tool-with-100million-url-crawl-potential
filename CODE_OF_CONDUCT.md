# Community Code of Conduct

SEO Screaming Toad welcomes people of every background and experience level. We want technical disagreement to be rigorous, useful, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share enough evidence for others to reproduce a claim.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, publication of private crawl data, or encouraging unauthorized crawling is not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens the community, the project, or other people. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed with discretion; no fixed response SLA is promised during the release-candidate stage.

This code applies in the repository and in public spaces when someone is representing the project.
